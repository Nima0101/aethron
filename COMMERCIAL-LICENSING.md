# AETHRON commercial licensing and pricing

AETHRON remains available under [GNU GPLv3](LICENSE) for everyone, including commercial users who comply with GPLv3. Organizations needing an **alternative licence for privately developed/proprietary integrations** can purchase rights to the AETHRON-owned portions of the software under a separate signed agreement. The GPL edition does not disappear when commercial agreements are signed.

**Published starting price schedule — 8 October 2026.** Prices are in EUR, **per year**, excluding any applicable VAT/taxes. These are standard indicative licence prices, not a checkout facility, unconditional legal offer or claim that AETHRON is production-qualified. A commercial licence becomes effective **only when both parties sign a completed order form and applicable agreement**. No licences, support subscriptions, OEM safety warranties or Founding slots are automatically activated by viewing this page.

**No standard per-device or per-camera royalty.** Product family means a distinct end-user offering or commercial integration; cosmetic variants, region codes and hardware-memory variants with the same essential product function count as one family. A separately sold software product with a materially different primary function counts separately. The signed order form specifies the actual licensed products and controls borderline cases. Developers include employees and external contractors who access, modify, compile or integrate licensed AETHRON source for the customer's product. End users and device operators are not developer seats.

**Eligibility:** Indie is for an individual or sole proprietor with one active developer. Startup is for a team of up to five developers; it is intended for early-stage businesses with combined prior-financial-year revenue and external funding raised in the preceding 12 months **below €1 million**, measured in EUR. Small Business and Business are available by size/scope, without a mandatory revenue threshold. When a customer outgrows a plan, upgrade at the next renewal or before beginning work that exceeds the contracted product/developer scope; deployment volume alone never forces an upgrade in standard tiers. OEM/Enterprise is **optional** when the customer needs a custom agreement, white-label redistribution/sublicensing, OEM-specific commitments or a negotiated warranty/SLA. A company integrating AETHRON into a car, drone or camera does not automatically owe an OEM fee merely because the end product contains hardware; safety and third-party rights remain separate.

### Founding Commercial Partner — first 25 signed customers

**€499/year fixed for three successive annual licence periods**, subject to timely payment and continued adherence to the agreed scope. The Founding plan includes **Small Business scope: up to 10 developers, 3 product families and unlimited deployments**, with licensed-version continuation rights described below. The customer signs a normal annual commercial licence; **the annual price is protected for three years**, not prepaid for all three years. New versions and updates are available during each paid annual term.

- Maximum **25** Founding commercial customer agreements across the programme; an enquiry or waitlist entry does **not** reserve a slot.
- A slot is allocated only after the maintainer manually confirms eligibility, provides an individually numbered order form and countersigns the executed agreement. No public counter, automatic checkout, public customer list or false availability claim is implied.
- Existing Founding customers keep €499 for the first three consecutive annual periods while paying on time and staying within 10 developers/3 product families. **After year 3**, renewal uses then-current published Small Business pricing unless separately agreed. No perpetual price lock.
- Upgrades beyond Founding scope, premium SLA, custom integrations or OEM requirements are quoted separately; these do not silently remove a customer's rights to already licensed versions.

**The Founding offer is a commercial policy, not evidence of existing customers.**

## What the commercial fee buys

- A **nonexclusive alternative copyright licence** for the specified AETHRON-owned source versions, named commercial product families and licensed developers; the customer may integrate those covered parts in proprietary applications and distribute the authorised product without the AETHRON GPL copyleft terms applying to the separately licensed parts.
- **Unlimited distribution/deployments** of the named product(s) within the signed order-form scope, with no mandatory activation server or unit reporting heartbeat.
- **Perpetual rights to the AETHRON-owned versions received during paid terms**, including distribution and maintenance of those versions in previously authorised products after renewal stops, unless terminated for material breach. No new AETHRON versions, added product families, premium services or support after the paid entitlement ends.
- Locally usable deployment: **no mandatory internet, cloud service, licence callback, forced account registration or connected developer computer for the deployed vehicle, drone or camera**. Administrative proof of commercial entitlement is contractual/offline, not a runtime safety dependency.
- Best-effort standard email support during an active paid term, **not** a guaranteed SLA or bespoke engineering engagement.

This licence **does not** grant another person's third-party models, SDKs, code, datasets or patents beyond rights held by the licensor. Customers remain responsible for obtaining those permissions and for product-specific safety qualification. The project is **research-stage, not certified or production-ready for automotive/UAS safety-critical use**. Commercial pricing and a signed licence are not evidence of operational qualification.

Standard licences do not include custom engineering, driver or camera compatibility guarantees, certified hardware, an embedded device, or a hosted service.

## Purchase and contracting workflow

1. Read the [pricing FAQ](docs/commercial/FAQ.md) and [licence agreement template](docs/commercial/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE-AGREEMENT-TEMPLATE.md).
2. Contact the maintainer through an **available private contact channel** on [the GitHub profile](https://github.com/Nima0101) with legal entity/country, plan, team size, intended product family, and whether the product is vehicle, UAS, home camera or software-only. Do **not** post confidential integration details publicly.
3. The maintainer verifies source ownership, contributor grants, third-party licence compatibility, product and support scope, and where appropriate Founding slot availability.
4. Parties approve a completed [order form](docs/commercial/ORDER-FORM-TEMPLATE.md) and agreement. The maintainer invoices the customer manually; no online payment processor or automatic licence issuance is represented as live.
5. After signatures and agreed payment, the owner sends the covered version identifiers and offline entitlement confirmation through the agreed private channel. No AETHRON runtime is dependent on this administrative process to continue operating.

See the [billing/support details](docs/commercial/SUPPORT-AND-BILLING.md), [owner's commercial operations checklist](docs/commercial/OWNER-OPERATIONS.md) and [dual-licensing scope](LICENSING.md). **This public price schedule is not a substitute for lawyer-reviewed, customer-specific contract terms.**

The [original GPLv3 licence](LICENSE), historical Apache-2.0 distribution rights, and third-party licensing obligations remain independent of the commercial programme.

# AETHRON commercial licensing

Copyright holder **Nima Khaki** controls the availability of separately negotiated commercial licences for the original AETHRON software for which the necessary rights are held.

The open-source edition continues under [GNU GPLv3](LICENSE) even when a separate commercial licence is issued to someone else.

## When alternative terms may be useful

Examples include proprietary OEM integrations, closed-source vehicle and drone applications, redistributed embedded appliances and enterprise systems that require a separate contract for source disclosure, sublicensing, support, liability or warranties.

**Commercial use by itself does not require a paid licence.** Businesses can use GPL-covered AETHRON software under GPL terms.

For private commercial licensing enquiries, contact the maintainer through an appropriate private channel listed on the [AETHRON GitHub profile](https://github.com/Nima0101). Do not post source code, secrets or negotiation terms in public issues.

Any commercial terms (licence scope, price, distribution and modification permissions, support, duration, territory and warranties) require an express signed agreement. **This page is neither an offer of fixed commercial terms nor a grant of proprietary rights.**

AETHRON's alternative grant cannot cover third-party code, SDKs, model weights, datasets or another contributor's additions without sufficient permission. Earlier Apache-2.0 grants for historical source versions continue to apply to copies distributed under them.

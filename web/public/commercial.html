No recurring device tax

Once commercially licensed, a named product family can be distributed to unlimited end users and devices under standard terms. Versions delivered during a paid term remain available for continued use and distribution in those products after nonrenewal, subject to the signed agreement. New releases and support require renewal.

Offline by design

No mandatory cloud activation, licensing callback, licence-server heartbeat, USB tether or developer laptop is imposed on an AETHRON device during normal local operation. This is the intended licensing and edge architecture, not evidence that a specific car, drone or camera integration is already qualified.

Support options

Standard commercial support: best effort while subscribed. Priority triage: €99/month (up to five requests, target reply within two Swedish business days). Custom integration engineering: €125–175/hour by separate agreement.

Request an agreement

Commercial licences are issued only by an individually signed order form and contract, followed by manual billing. There is no automatic checkout. Contact the maintainer using a verified private channel on the GitHub profile. Do not share confidential business details in public issues.

Licensing is not safety certification

AETHRON is research-stage. A commercial licence does not certify autonomous driving, drone operation, dark-condition accuracy, OEM camera access, product safety or regulatory approval. Third-party datasets, weights, SDKs and libraries retain their own licences. Previously granted Apache-2.0 rights are not revoked.

The prices above are a published reference schedule, not a binding offer of a particular product, warranty, safety claim, Founding slot or service. Proprietary rights arise only upon a completed and executed licence agreement.

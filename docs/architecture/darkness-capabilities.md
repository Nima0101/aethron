# Darkness capability amendment
| Adapter | Daylight | Low light | Near/zero visible | Rectangles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RGB minimized cue | conditional | withdraw | withdraw | never human |
| Passive depth | conditional | withdraw | withdraw | only verified non-human |
| Active depth | conditional | conditional | conditional | only verified non-human, direct view |
| Radiometric thermal | conditional | conditional | conditional | only verified non-human, direct view |
| Radar coarse occupancy | conditional | conditional | conditional | never |
All conditions additionally require calibration, same clock, freshness and valid quality. Active illumination interference, reflectivity, thermal saturation, multipath and occlusion still invalidate. Reference mode is synthetic or unverified external observations; zero devices are live-qualified. Thermal hot/cold/fire-like classifications are rule-based temperature anomalies, not person recognition or confirmed fire. No raw person image or radar data is accepted. Phone/vehicle/drone interfaces are integration fixtures until exact devices/controllers pass H02/H03.

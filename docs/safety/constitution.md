# Safety constitution v1 — immutable minimums
PROHIBITED: biometric identification; face, gait or voice recognition; persistent person re-identification; stable cross-scene person IDs; covert person tracking; target designation; person threat scoring; autonomous person pursuit, chasing or following; weapon integration; firing or engagement decisions; through-wall person imagery or reconstruction; hidden high-resolution localization behind barriers; precise behind-wall trajectories; vital-sign extraction; raw exports or APIs that defeat these restrictions; surveillance of named or identifiable individuals. Do not implement, expose, enable or document operational routes to these capabilities.
Allowed: transient human presence without identity; hot/cold/fire-like thermal sources; supported smoke/haze cues; coarse obstacles/free-space; authorized coarse through-obstruction zone presence with uncertainty; non-identifying hazard summaries; local warnings; STOP/HOVER/LAND/RETREAT recommendations under a declared controller contract. No action pursues people. Never output SAFE; UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN. No confidence laundering or smoothing that hides failures.
Tightened v1 boundary: no human bounding boxes, counts, global coordinates, arbitrary zone names, raw image/radar export, audio input, retained sensor history, online learning, generic external executable model loaders or direct actuator output. Through-obstruction observations require explicit local authorization and a fixed coarse zone vocabulary. Authorization is a caller assertion, not proof of legal authority. Precise depth samples may only become coarse obstacle status; discard input after evaluation. Thermal source classes do not establish ignition or fire diagnosis. A sensor may be wrong even at high confidence.

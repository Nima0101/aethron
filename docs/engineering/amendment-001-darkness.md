# Amendment 001 — darkness and non-human rectangles
Additive owner update, frozen 2026-10-08 before production source. Read with AGENTS.md; original hashes remain unchanged.
No RGB dependency: daylight, low_light, near_dark and zero_visible are explicit input conditions. Visible camera and passive-depth claims withdraw in near_dark/zero_visible; low_light camera claims withdraw conservatively in this reference. Thermal, radar and declared active depth can contribute only with valid quality, calibration and freshness. Technology capability is not a live-device validation claim.
Non-human rectangles require line-of-sight, validated sensor-plane localization, explicit non-human exclusion evidence, quantized normalized coordinates, allowed class, uncertainty and expiry. No rectangles for human cues or radar/through-obstruction; no identity, object IDs, tracks or frame-to-frame links. If human exclusion is ambiguous, omit the rectangle even when a coarse hazard remains. Merely hot pixels never prove non-human. Device adapters need independent exclusion validation; caller declarations cannot establish physical truth.

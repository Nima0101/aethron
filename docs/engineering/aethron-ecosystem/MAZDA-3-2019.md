# Mazda 3 Sport 2019 — one feasibility case

Status: **not hardware-tested; no verified OEM video API**. “Sport” is market-dependent. Confirm hatchback/sedan, regional specification, trim, infotainment firmware, actual camera equipment and accessory electrical ratings before buying an interface. The retrieved 2019 regional Mazda manual/search evidence describes rear/360-view equipment [S03](SOURCES.md#s03); it does not document a third-party raw-video entitlement. The large US sedan PDF could not be fully retrieved and is not proof about a Canadian/European Sport configuration.

## OEM route decision

Ask the authorized vehicle/SDK provider for a documented image export and integration permissions for the exact equipment. Record transport, format, capture timestamp, supported vehicle states and warranty/safety conditions. Without those, mark OEM-camera integration **unverified/unsupported**. OBD-II, CAN, USB and Mazda Connect/CarPlay presence do not imply a camera stream. Do not splice the reversing camera, bypass head-unit protections or disturb ADAS calibration as a default recipe.

## Credible retrofit route

1. Confirm owner authority and intended stationary/closed-site observation use; document mounting/privacy conditions.
2. Start with an independently powered laptop or mini-PC and external UVC camera on a bench. Use a data-capable cable and a camera with documented OS formats. Validate the future installed edge package through licensed replay first.
3. On the parked vehicle, use a secure unobstructive daylight camera mount. Keep it clear of windshield ADAS sensors, airbags and the driver's view. Route cables clear of all controls. Record pose and image calibration.
4. For true zero visible light, add LWIR with exterior weather protection and appropriate thermal optics, or an explicitly supported active NIR/depth/radar rig. Do not place LWIR behind ordinary windshield glass and claim an external thermal view [S22](SOURCES.md#s22).
5. Use the exact vehicle manual's accessory outlet rating and an appropriately protected converter, or keep the independent battery for early tests. Measure drain, brownout, boot/recovery, ambient temperature and thermal throttling. A qualified installer designs any fixed wiring; no fuse-tap/pinout guess is part of this plan.
6. Configure separate daylight and nonvisible source profiles; acquire clock/calibration evidence. Show UNKNOWN before valid support and immediately expire stale outputs after unplug/cover/sleep tests.
7. Record measured SKU compatibility and operating domain, then conduct an authorized controlled trial with an independent observer. Public-road collision-avoidance or safety claims require further system assurance; successful preview is insufficient.

## Expected capability and unresolved facts

The retrofit route can deliver locally processed observation once implemented and tested, without factory camera access. Phone app is a display/management client or its own qualified source, not a universal Mazda camera bridge. The existing PWA can preview an authorized phone/device camera; it is not a zero-light retrofit by itself.

Open facts: exact market/trim, installed OEM camera system, mounting legality, outlet ratings, available hardware, nonvisible optics, clock quality, computer thermal behavior and supported phone accessory path. These block exact hardware qualification, not Phase 1 adapter/replay/service work. Reuse the same decision tree for other vehicles; do not add Mazda-specific assumptions to the core API.

## Permanent installation completion

The laptop/portable-power steps are commissioning only. Normal daily use follows the [permanent vehicle appliance recipe](VEHICLE-COMPATIBILITY.md): fixed compact compute, approved protected supply, secured sensor wiring and independent local status, with a signed service/image starting on vehicle/accessory power. Remove phone/laptop/provisioning USB host, disable WAN and power-cycle the installed unit. A01–A09 must demonstrate local inference/expiry/recovery without user commands. Exact mounting/power/device approval remains unresolved for this Sport trim; no proprietary factory-camera promise is added.

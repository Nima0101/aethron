# Engineering constitution v1
Frozen before production source, 2026-10-08.
Mission: portable, privacy-minimizing rescue perception and bounded safety recommendations. Sensor truth is not established by schema validity. Determinism, finite resource limits, freshness, calibration and explicit uncertainty precede presentation. Separate adapters, time/calibration validation, feature extraction, fusion, bounded outputs and controller contracts. No unreviewed generic detector/plugin escape hatch. Offline operation requires no credentials, service or network. Do not pretend to certify a safety system.
The owner authorization safety floor is reproduced in the safety constitution. It cannot be weakened by configuration, amendments or integration convenience. Freeze hashes before source; keep original files immutable. Architecture protocol freezes after primary-source research. Later changes require versioning and retained counterexamples. Every claim points to a check and its limits. Same-author review is not independent assurance. Continue independent work when a gate is externally blocked.

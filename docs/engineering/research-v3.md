# v3 primary-source review — 2026-10-08
Research separates commercial assertions from mechanisms we can test. No third-party reference images are redistributed and no endorsement is implied.

|---|---|
| [Visage UAV PoC](https://visagetechnologies.com/blog/uav-detection/) (28 April2026) | Thermal small-object detection, L2/IoU association, motion filtering and compensation are useful hypotheses. Solar loading, small pixel footprints and missing monocular depth are explicit limitations. Their range, detector threshold and performance assertions are not ours. SUAVE-600 v3 licensing could not be independently established. |

We read public abstracts, not paid full standards; no compliance/certification claim. Device/controller safety case remains external. |

Differentiation is inspectable bounded state, honest degradation and reproducibility, not a novelty or superiority claim. Physical day/night detection, thermal semantic accuracy, power and controller safety are not supplied by tracking software. Original baseline detector and parameter policy require model cards even without learned weights. Every acquired excerpt gets source URL, license, transform, byte hashes and a held-out/evaluation-only declaration. Frozen evaluation data must never become training data while retaining the same reported test status.

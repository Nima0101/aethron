# AETHRON commercial licensing FAQ

[Pricing and purchase workflow](../../COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md) · [Draft commercial agreement](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE-AGREEMENT-TEMPLATE.md) · [GPLv3 community licence](../../LICENSE)

**Can a business use AETHRON without paying?** Yes. GPLv3 permits commercial and internal business use, subject to its conditions. Payment buys alternative rights, not permission to be a business. Distribution of GPL-covered modified software can trigger corresponding-source duties; seek legal advice for a particular combined work.

**Is the price per camera, vehicle, drone or customer?** No. Standard commercial prices are yearly per developer/product entitlement and allow unlimited installations of licensed products. There is no device-count heartbeat, unit royalty or per-camera tax. Developers do not include end users or people merely operating the end product.

**What is a developer seat?** A named employee or contractor with direct AETHRON source access or responsibility for modifying, compiling or integrating the separately licensed source. A seat can be reassigned permanently when a developer leaves or changes roles; it is not a concurrent-use pool.

**What counts as a product family/SKU?** A distinct externally marketed or internally deployed offering with substantially the same primary functionality. Cosmetic, regional, memory or packaging variants of one essential product family count together. Separately marketed offerings with materially different primary functions count separately. List the real names in the signed order form and resolve uncertain classifications before deployment.

**Must automotive, UAV and smart-camera vendors purchase OEM instead of Startup/Business?** No. They may use an appropriate standard plan where no bespoke OEM redistribution, sublicensing, support or warranty terms are needed. Safety, installation, hardware permissions and regulatory obligations remain irrespective of plan. OEM starts at €4,900 annually per product family when individually negotiated.

**What does 'unlimited deployment' really mean?** Unlimited devices and end users for the named products while the contractual developer/product-family scope and other licence requirements are satisfied. It does not allow selling AETHRON itself as an unrelated standalone SDK, reselling licences or sublicensing source rights without consent.

**Why buy commercial if GPLv3 is free?** A valid separate licence can allow proprietary integrations/distribution under contractual terms without GPL copyleft obligations for the specific AETHRON-owned material covered. It cannot remove other parties' GPL obligations or third-party model, SDK, data or patent restrictions.

**Can a customer keep using and selling versions after cancelling renewal?** Yes, for the identified AETHRON-owned versions delivered during a paid term and the already authorised product families, subject to continuing contract terms and termination for material breach. Maintenance of that product with those versions remains permitted. No future AETHRON versions, additional distinct products or ongoing support are included after expiry.

**Does unpaid renewal shut down installed vehicles or drones?** No. AETHRON must not require online activation, a licence server, network access or remote subscription heartbeat for local inference. Contract disputes are handled through contract remedies, not live shutdown.

**How does Founding work?** Up to 25 signed partners may receive Small Business scope (10 developers, 3 product families) at €499 per year for three consecutive annual paid periods. A quote/request does not reserve a slot. The owner allocates a numbered slot only on a countersigned agreement. Year four is renewed at then-current Small Business rates unless separately agreed. No existing slots are claimed as occupied.

**What if we grow?** Request a documented plan amendment before adding active source developers or separate product families beyond your entitlement; upgrades may be prorated by agreement. Volume alone never forces an upgrade.

**Are payments, subscriptions and licences automatically provisioned?** No. Public prices are a reference schedule. A signed agreement plus accepted order form and agreed payment are required. Billing and entitlement issuance are manual until a proper system is actually built and verified.

**What support is included?** Best-effort standard email support during paid terms, no SLA. Optional priority support is €99 per month for up to five requests/month with a *target* response within two Swedish business days, not a guaranteed fix. Custom engineering is quoted separately at an indicative €125–175/hour.

**Is AETHRON production-ready and certified for safety-critical driving or flights?** No. Neither an alternative licence nor payment constitutes platform availability, verified night detection, field validation, automotive/UAS certification or operational approval. Any production-critical use needs its own compatible hardware, deployment qualification and legal review.

**Will historical Apache-2.0 releases lose rights?** No. Recipients keep the licence permissions already granted for those historical versions. New public versions follow the current GNU GPLv3 grant for applicable project-owned code.

**Can a customer change someone else's open-source model licence via AETHRON commercial terms?** No. Third-party models, library code, SDKs and datasets retain their own conditions and may require additional permission or replacement.

**Who owns changes?** Customer-written, independently owned modifications remain the customer's property, subject to underlying AETHRON copyright. Rights to separately contributed code need express agreements before the maintainer can commercially relicense it.

**Can a customer buy a licence immediately through this repository?** Not yet. Use a verifiable private contact channel found on the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Nima0101), get a completed offer and signed documents, then a manual invoice. Do not publish confidential engineering plans or banking information in an issue. Pricing availability does not imply that a particular API, OEM adapter or firmware currently exists.

# Support, billing and operational expectations

This is the default commercial service policy for the pricing schedule in [COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md](../../COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md). It is a proposal and must be incorporated into a **signed order** to be binding. It does not represent an already staffed 24/7 support desk.

## Support catalogue

| Entitlement | Price (EUR excluding VAT) | Scope | Response |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Community | €0 | Public issue templates/docs/peer assistance | No promise |
| Standard commercial support | Included during paid annual term | Routine installation, licensing, version and public-documentation questions via an agreed private email channel | Best effort; no SLA |
| Optional priority support | €99/month | Maximum five new support requests per monthly period; business-hours triage in Swedish business week | Target initial human reply within two business days; not a guarantee |
| Engineering assistance | €125–175/hour, agreed before work | Troubleshooting, integrating permitted APIs and custom adapters | SOW with estimate and agreed rate |
| OEM, certification assistance, dedicated service | Quote | Device-specific integration, onsite work, compliance artefacts or SLA | Separate signed SOW |

Business hours are Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00 Europe/Stockholm excluding Swedish public holidays. First response is acknowledgement/triage, **not** a fix or restoration promise. Security incidents should be reported privately using the current [security reporting policy](../../SECURITY.md); no guaranteed incident response is promised unless a separate SLA says so.

## Exclusions

Support does not include unattended production monitoring, hosted availability, 24/7 on-call, OEM camera unlocks, safety sign-off, field tests, device manufacturing, custom firmware, vendor API keys, patent licences, hardware procurement or uncapped engineering. The owner may decline unlawful, unsafe or technically unavailable integrations. Defects in a field-installed safety product are not transformed into certified capabilities by opening a support ticket.

## Annual fees and renewal

- The prices listed are **per 12-month entitlement period** in EUR, before any applicable tax/VAT. Licence issuance and renewal are **manual by signed order and invoice**, not an operational automated subscription service.
- Default invoice term: 30 calendar days from invoice date unless stated otherwise. The effective annual term and delivered code versions are recorded on the signed order form. No credit-card autocharge or recurring direct debit has been set up.
- No automatic renewal unless explicitly and separately agreed in writing. At renewal, the owner supplies the next year's quote and a new accepted order; an unauthorised price increase cannot change perpetual rights to previously covered versions.
- Pricing may change for **new** contracts. A Founding customer who stays within scope and pays each annual order on time is protected at €499/year for the first three consecutive annual periods, after which the then-current Small Business price applies unless differently agreed.
- Upgrades/additional developer seats or products require a written addendum/order. Any proration of the remaining term must be approved by both parties; it is not a secret usage-based bill. Deploying additional devices in the same covered product generates no device bill.
- The owner issues licence or entitlement confirmations offline, documenting customer, products, effective term, version entitlement, plan and agreement ID. There is **no licence-server connection or forced online activation** inside an AETHRON running in a vehicle, drone or smart camera.
- VAT/tax treatment depends on jurisdiction, business status and registration; the licensor should verify VAT registration, invoice contents and reverse-charge treatment with qualified local advisers before sales. Prices exclude taxes and do not promise VAT-free sales.
- Refund/cancellation terms must be explicit in the executed agreement and consistent with mandatory consumer law if it applies. There is no claim of automated instant refunds or checkout.

## Perpetual-version licence versus support renewal

Paid-up identified versions and their approved product integrations remain licensable/distributable after lapse of the annual *updates/support entitlement*, subject to material-breach termination and contract conditions. The AETHRON runtime will not stop or phone home. Newly released versions, additional product families, new contracts and continuing support require an active entitlement.

## Security and personal-data handling

No automatic upload of camera feeds, person detections, vehicle routes or device telemetry is required for sales/support. The customer should redact secrets, personally identifying data and surveillance material from support reports. A specific data-processing agreement may be needed if the licensor becomes a processor; **none is provided merely by buying a licence**.

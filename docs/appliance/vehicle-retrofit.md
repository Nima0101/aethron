# Vehicle retrofit operator guide — candidate

Your installer supplies permanently secured compute, authorized sensors and protected low-voltage power. A laptop or phone is not part of everyday operation. Power on the installed unit; its supervisor starts locally. Check the installed indicator before relying on any information. Startup, stale status, darkness without a matching sensor, or a fault means UNKNOWN. It does not control brakes or steering.

If a fault persists, park safely and request service. Do not loosen camera mounts, alter calibration, bypass fuses, reconnect OEM ADAS wiring or install downloaded weights yourself. Optional local diagnostics are for maintenance. Losing WAN or closing the phone must not stop the device.

The common Linux boot/offline test exercises the software recipe with simulated sensors. No particular vehicle, power supply, enclosure, mount, ignition lifecycle or thermal range is qualified here. Installer commissioning includes the vehicle-specific power/mount/privacy checklist and real sensor-loss tests. Mazda 3 Sport 2019 is one potential retrofit case, not universal OEM camera support.
